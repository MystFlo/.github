## MystFlo

**On-demand AI assistants that run the business side for you, so you can focus on your craft.**

Pre-built. 24/7. Run your business like a 10-person team.

MystFlo is in private beta. Want in early? Join the waitlist at [mystflo.com](https://mystflo.com).

---

### Who it's for

Founders with real traction but no repeatable system. People already reach out, apply, book, and pay — but it all runs on your memory, a messy inbox, and half-documented steps. Every launch, you're playing catch-up instead of scaling what works.

Think retreat hosts, makers, and experience hosts — you create the value AND run the operations. MystFlo handles the operator side so you can focus on the creator side.

---

### The Brain

Every assistant runs on a Brain that knows your business. It arrives pre-seeded with how businesses like yours already run, then you confirm and correct the specifics — your voice, prices, policies, FAQs. Everything an assistant says sounds like you — because you confirmed it does.

Teach it once. It doesn't churn, doesn't need managing, doesn't forget. A human assistant takes weeks to train, and when they quit the knowledge walks out. Your Brain stays.

---

### Not auto-replies. Operations.

Answering a DM is easy — plenty of tools do that. MystFlo assistants take the conversation and do the operation across your tools: take the booking, chase the deposit, open the waitlist, send reminders that know who paid and who didn't.

We feed the tools you already use — never replace them. WhatsApp, Google Workspace, Stripe, Slack, Shopify, Calendly, Airtable, Booking.com, and more. Connect once, run forever.

### How it works

```
Explore  →  Connect  →  Activate  →  Pulse
```

**1. Explore** — Browse assistants by outcome. Watch a demo before you commit.

**2. Connect** — Link your apps once. Your credentials stay encrypted, always under your control.

**3. Activate** — Preview with a test run. See exactly what happens before going live.

**4. Pulse** — Monitor runs, success rates, and spend. Pause and resume on your business's rhythm.

---

### Pricing

**Pay per run.** No subscription. No monthly fee. When nothing runs — between launches, in the off-season — you pay nothing.

Set a monthly budget as a safety line: with auto-pause on, assistants stop when they hit it. No surprises.

AI runs on MystFlo by default — paid per run, no key to set up. Or connect your own AI key — those costs go straight to your account and you pay only the per-run fee. App costs (WhatsApp, Stripe) go to your connected accounts either way.
