## MystFlo

**MystFlo builds AI agents for businesses — chat, booking and payment assistants — and runs them for you.**

AI helps. MystFlo does the work.

### Who it's for

Businesses whose customers message them to book or order — retreats, workshops, studios, cafés, hosts — and who still run it from memory and one inbox.

---

### The Brain

Every hosted assistant answers from one Brain: your prices, policies, offers, FAQs and voice, across every part of your business. Nothing goes into it until you approve it, and each fact you confirm is in the next run. It sounds like you because you approved it.

Already told your AI about your business? Run the MystFlo prompt from [mystflo.com](https://mystflo.com) in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok and bring the answer in to confirm. No key, no connector.

Next: every agent you own reads the same Brain, your own Claude or Grok setup included. Later: it learns from every run. Coming, not shipped.

### Pricing

Each build is quoted after a short call. What runs on MystFlo is paid per run from a prepaid wallet, with no subscription; when nothing runs, nothing is charged. App costs (WhatsApp, Stripe) go to your own connected accounts.
