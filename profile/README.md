## MystFlo

**AI helps. MystFlo does the work.**

MystFlo is the AI operations platform for creative and wellness founders. The Brain agent organises your retreats, workshops, events and products into one Brain you confirm. AI assistants do the work from it — replies, bookings, deposits, listing copy — in the tools you already use.

Private beta. Interested founders and businesses can request an invite at [mystflo.com](https://mystflo.com).

---

### The problem

AI drafts. The booking, the deposit, the listing still run through you — and every tool you add starts from zero on your business. MystFlo closes that gap two ways: one Brain for every stream, and assistants that read it and do the work.

---

### Who it's for

Creative and wellness founders — makers, artists, hosts of retreats, trips, workshops and events — whose craft already pays through several of these streams, and who run all of it from memory and one inbox, with no place that holds how the business runs.

---

### The Brain agent

The Brain agent builds your Brain with you. It researches your business, asks what's missing, and drafts your prices, policies and voice for you to confirm — so your assistants are ready faster. Every correction you confirm is in every assistant's next run. It sounds like you because you confirmed it does.

Next: it learns from every run and gets your assistants sharper the more you use them. Coming, not shipped.

### Works with every AI provider

Already told your AI about your business? Copy the MystFlo prompt on [mystflo.com](https://mystflo.com), run it in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok, and bring the answer into your Brain to confirm. MystFlo never connects to them: no key, no connector.

Connects today: WhatsApp, Google Workspace, Stripe and Slack.

---

### How it works

Assistants → Personalize → Connect → Try → Pulse

1. **Assistants** — browse the shelf. Each card shows the job, the apps it needs and the price per run.
2. **Personalize** — the Brain agent drafts your Brain; you confirm prices, policies and how you talk.
3. **Connect** — link the one app the job needs. Credentials are encrypted and stay under your control.
4. **Try** — a test run before anything goes live.
5. **Pulse** — every run, every cost, in one place. Pause, resume, or stop anytime.

---

### Pricing

Pay only when it runs. No subscription, no minimum. When nothing runs — between launches, in the off-season — you pay nothing. App costs (WhatsApp, Stripe) go to your connected accounts. AI runs on MystFlo, paid per run, no key to set up.
